On a bright, cloudy day in Pune, two 10-year-old childhood best friends, Arav and Kabir, stand together on the terrace of Arav's middle-class home. Across the road, three tall residential towers are under construction, side by side. In the first shot, the boys face the camera, looking out at the towers, which are behind the camera and not yet seen. Kabir has his arm around Arav's shoulder. Arav dreamily points out at the towers and says that one day he will have his own home in a tall building like that. Kabir turns to him and asks what kind of home. Arav, getting excited, says it will be big, with a separate room for everyone, one for Mummy-Papa and one for Dadi. We cut to the second shot, one seamless handheld take. It opens tight on Arav over Kabir's shoulder as Arav puts his hand on his chest and says, "and mine too." Arav breaks away and walks to the parapet with the camera following close behind him, and as he reaches the railing, the three under-construction towers are revealed in front of him for the first time. Arav turns back to Kabir with the towers standing tall behind him, and talks about big windows, European style, just like in the movies. Kabir says "aacha", and Arav puts his hands on his waist, proud, and says "haan". Arav steps closer and says his society will even have a race course. The camera whips to Kabir, who reacts in disbelief and asks who would ever build a home like that. The camera whips back to Arav in close-up, the towers soft behind him. He looks at the towers in silence, says "koi to" still looking at them, pauses, then turns to Kabir and says "banata hi hoga" with a face full of hope.
